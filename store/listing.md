האפליקציה תומכת בתרגול בבית. היא לא מחליפה קלינאית תקשורת.

Talki helps toddlers practice first Hebrew words. Tap a picture, hear the word, say it back. Games and speech exercises stay on the device — no account, no server. A parent can record a familiar voice and add family words.

## App Store

**Name:** Talki

**Promotional text:** לוחצים, שומעים ולומדים מילים ראשונות בעברית — גם בלי אינטרנט.
**Keywords:** Hebrew,speech,toddler,words,kids,עברית,דיבור,פעוטות,מילים,למידה

**Description:**
Talki is a Hebrew early-speech practice app for toddlers.

Tap a picture to hear the word, then repeat it. Flashcards, memory and matching games, plus speech exercises modeled on techniques used with late talkers: focused stimulation, receptive identification, cloze/expectant delay, communication temptation, and minimal pairs.

Everything stays on the device. No account, no cloud, no internet required after install. Parents can record a real voice for each word, add personal words, and export a backup file.

Talki supports home practice. It does not replace a speech-language pathologist.
